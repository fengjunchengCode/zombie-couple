# Zombie Couple

Notes and free prompts for the zombie couple video trend: one person turns into a zombie, the other raises a gun, can't fire, lowers it, and they hug. The format took off on TikTok in early October 2026.

## Make one from two photos

[Zombie Couple](https://zombie-couple.com/) takes a photo of the one who turns and a photo of the one holding the gun, and renders the scene as a 10-second video.

- About 5 minutes per render
- 9:16, 16:9 or 1:1
- Sound included, no watermark
- If a render fails, the credits go back automatically
- Uploaded photos are deleted after 14 days

## Notes from testing

- The model is Seedance 2.0. Test renders took 274 and 388 seconds.
- The first test render gave the zombie bloody tears. Adding "no bloody tears" to the prompt fixed it.
- Clear, front-facing photos of both people work best.
